    AUTOMATIC BREAKS OFF

    Off by default. When enabled, the timer runs while Saturday Tube is open, on every input, even while paused or buffering. All channels, VHS and SAT pause during breaks and resume from the same point. You can end a break early.

    Placement moves the room behind the stationary TV. Each room remembers its own placement.

    DEVELOPER TOOL

    ORIGINAL runs one way: zoom in to the first screen-fit edge, or zoom out to reveal black. BOOMERANG keeps the TV centered, holds 10 seconds at screen size, zooms back to the room-image edge without black, waits 3 seconds, then repeats. Other framing controls lock while it runs; room and lighting choices remain available.

    OFF

    Screen height changes only the picture area; controls and VHS deck stay fixed. Video fits automatically without stretching. Each room remembers its own TV placement; size and height stay shared. All settings survive restarts. Drag to move the whole room; scroll to zoom. Esc leaves.
