These locally bundled photographs are retained as licensed visual references; the current interface does not display them. The following licensed files are retained in the source tree for possible future use. They are excluded from the packaged app and are not used by this release.

- **Analog television static picture** — Matthew Paul Argall, [Internet Archive: Static/noise (snow) on old analog TV set](https://archive.org/details/StaticnoisesnowOnOldAnalogTvSet), licensed [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
