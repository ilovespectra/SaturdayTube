Make it a Saturday

A familiar set, made yours.

A thoughtfully local player for the video you already love—wrapped in a cabinet that feels like home.

▤

Bring your own tapes

Import video into your shelf, or point channels at your own folders. Your channel-source files stay where you put them.

◉

Dial in your picture

Shape the CRT with scanlines, curvature, VHS tracking and other picture effects. Pick a cabinet finish that feels right.

⌁

Shuffle without repeats

Movies and other loose channel videos don’t repeat until that pool is exhausted. Shuffle Lineups reshuffles upcoming programmes across channels without changing episode order within a series.

YouTube is optional and uses its official embedded player. Sign-in, availability and advertising remain under YouTube’s control.
